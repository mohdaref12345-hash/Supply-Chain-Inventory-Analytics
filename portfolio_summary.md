# Portfolio Summary

## Supply Chain & Inventory Analytics
**Tools:** Python (Pandas), PostgreSQL, SQL, Excel, Power BI (report recreation guide)

Analyzed a daily supply-chain dataset containing 91,250 observations across 50 SKUs, 5 warehouses, 10 suppliers, and 4 regions. Developed inventory and commercial KPIs, including sales value, COGS, gross profit, gross margin, reorder-threshold exposure, stockout observations, and supplier lead-time comparisons. Added reusable SQL analytical views and data-quality checks, and prepared a dashboard concept to compare SKU, warehouse, supplier, and regional performance.

**Key result:** 4,787 observations (approximately 5.25% of all records) had inventory below the dataset's reorder point. This is an observation-level replenishment risk indicator, not a forecast or confirmation of a future stockout.

**Data source:** Public GitHub project dataset. This portfolio work builds on the published source and does not claim ownership of the original dataset/project.
